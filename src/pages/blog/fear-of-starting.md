## Fear is normal
Feeling stupid at the start means you’re learning something worth learning.

## Lower the bar
Aim for:
> Make it work, not make it perfect.

## Time box
Work 20 minutes. Stop. Repeat.

## Confidence formula
Exposure + repetition = competence → confidence

## Reminder
You don’t need permission to start.
