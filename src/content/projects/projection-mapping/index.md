## The brief

WONDER is a discovery trail through design, technology, art and innovation in Kortrijk, UNESCO City of Design, from 27 October to 8 November 2026. Every evening during the festival, the belfry on the Grote Markt becomes a canvas for projected visuals.

The belfry is a medieval bell tower in the historic centre of Kortrijk and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. It once held the city’s treasury and archives, and served as a lookout for fires and other danger.

Since 2017, Kortrijk has been part of the UNESCO Creative Cities of Design network. Its partner cities of design were the inspiration for the final-year students of Devine at Howest. Our team’s city was Helsinki.

## The story

The piece follows the seasons into Helsinki. It opens on a frozen tower, with a bear in a nightcap and a hot drink at the window. The ice cracks, the stone turns green and flowers grow over the walls.

Then the belfry changes shape: a blue tower with a red spire, a dome, an arch, each drawn in flat colour on the tower’s own outline. Finally the sea rises over the stone and carries off a ship flying Helsinki’s flag, with our names in the waves.

## One week

We had five days, from Monday 12 to Friday 16 October, to go from the brief to the final render. Maria, Gabriela and Nabilah drew the illustrations; Idris, Maria and I animated them.

The render is 1182 × 3500 pixels and follows the outline of the belfry, so the colour lands on the stone and the sky around the tower stays dark. Flat colour and clear silhouettes still read on the rough brick from across the square.

## On the belfry

Urban Mapping provided the technical support. The piece plays continuously every evening of WONDER, from 27 October to 8 November 2026.
