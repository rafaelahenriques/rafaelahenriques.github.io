## The idea

The Ben & Jerry’s pint already suggests a world of blue skies, white clouds and dairy farming. I wanted to build that world on top of the ice cream itself, so the product and the story are one object. The format was inspired by Campari’s website, where a drink gets made in front of you.

## How it was made

I started from a farm diorama I had modelled in Maya, and brought the barn, trees, tractor, hay and sunflowers into a new Blender file. The pint, lid, milk can, scoop, ice cream, fences and low-poly cows were built new for this project.

## Making it read as ice cream

Early versions didn’t look right, so I rebuilt the surface from overlapping scoops with scraped layers and a torn texture. The material also changes from glossy liquid to frozen as the cream swells.

## Simplifying the farm

Early layouts were crowded with a house, windmill, pig, chickens, a coop and pumpkins. I cut these back so the barn is the clear focal point, then placed the final elements by hand.

## Believable animals

The cows have separate neck and head joints, so they graze naturally and their heads stop just above the ice cream.

## Designed for the page

The background matches the site’s blue, the brand colours render without a filmic shift, and the last frame matches the first so the loop has no visible cut.
